RabbitMQ is starting up. It may take a few minutes for all nodes to become ready.

Check the status of the StatefulSet:
  kubectl get statefulset --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "rabbitmq.fullname" . }}

Access the management UI by port-forwarding:
  kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "rabbitmq.fullname" . }} 15672:15672

Then open http://localhost:15672 in your browser (default credentials: user / <password from secret>).

Retrieve the password:
  kubectl get secret --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "rabbitmq.fullname" . }} -o jsonpath="{.data.password}" | base64 -d
